## Verified October 31 pivot: individual adapters → mechanics families → declarative recipes

**2026-11-07 forensic answer to owner:** YES, the recollection is supported by the actual dated sources, with three separate layers often called "effects" in past discussions. The owner-approved pivot is explicitly dated **2026-10-31** and was recorded in [Tactical Spell Mechanics Review](Tactical_Spell_Mechanics_Review_2026-10-31.md), [Composable Spell Engine with Human + Actor AI](Composable_Spell_Engine_AI_Readiness_2026-10-31.md), [Spell Engine Pre-pivot Audit](Spell_Engine_PrePivot_Audit_2026-10-31.md) and [six owner decisions](SPELL_ENGINE_DECISIONS.md). It **did not replace, uninstall or finish migrating** the earlier deployed per-spell adapters.

**Chronology and distinction:**
1. **July 6 — spell database import, NOT execution:** [Spell / Magic System Foundation](Spell_Magic_System_Foundation.md), \`spells_catalog\` + \`spell_effects\` + \`character_spells\`, metadata normalization and controlled importer. The \`spell_effects\` rows are **descriptive effect records** intended as a later building block, not effect graphs or executable engine nodes.
2. **July 27–30 — individual, executable tactical spell adapters:** Phase 1I first granted two narrow cast paths (Fire Bolt/Cure Wounds), then Phase 1J–1Z built out the 19 XPHB combat-page whitelist with specific server versions \`encounter_cast_spell_v1..v13\` plus area/point/dart/directional RPCs, reusable damage/heal/conditions/timing and live smoke/rollback tests. Those old RPCs **still exist on current main/live SQL**. They are test oracles, not future repeated authoring strategy.
3. **October 23–25 — separate generic CHARACTER effects arithmetic research:** held #251→#253→#255 tested a private immutable numeric/roll-mode resolver and 16 original structured equipment source subsets. **Not the tactical spell recipe compiler, not installed on main**, and not entitled to spend combat resources.
4. **October 29–30 — scaling problem exposed:** held Eldritch Blast / Agonizing Blast added source-bound per-beam attacks, repeat target allocation, full combat/RNG/action/replay nuances and meaningful isolated Auth/source testing. Scorching Ray, Ice Knife, reaction spells, concentration and mixed/delayed effects demonstrate why continually adding one spell-specific RPC does not scale to the catalogue.
5. **October 31 — explicit owner-approved architecture pivot:** source catalogue audit saw **1,037 spell rows from 18 sources, 391 XPHB spells**, 24 XPHB catalogue attack-type signals, 154 save signals, 34 area signals, 23 healing signals. **Overlapping/incomplete metadata**, NOT cast families that can be auto-promoted. Fireball's \`area_type\` was null despite its area description. Decision: classify by **mechanic/execution family** only for review, then author **source-qualified versioned declarative spell recipes** from a CLOSED set of trusted geometry/targets, attack/save/auto-hit, damage/healing, repeat/branch/roll-scope, resources and lifecycle primitives. Do not handwrite hundreds of independent RPCs, do not auto-execute all imported \`spell_effects\`, do not create a parallel generic modifier/combat state engine. A future Admin visual recipe builder and same-legal-actions Actor AI are separately approved design *goals*, not live features.
6. **Oct 31 / Nov 1 held source implementation:** PR [#309](https://github.com/bob8675309/DNDNEXT/pull/309) contains \`utils/tacticalSpellMechanicsReview.mjs\` + validator (24 manually enumerated XPHB attack entries, **22 explicit special attack exceptions**, 8 other special shapes; status always manual-review/not cast-approved), \`utils/spellRecipes/validateSpellRecipe.mjs\` (P1 closed structural schema with **seven node ops** \`attack\`, \`save\`, \`damage\`, \`heal\`, \`area\`, \`repeat\`, \`auto-hit\`), \`utils/spellRecipes/preflightSpellRecipe.mjs\` (immutable SHA256 recipe fingerprint, roll-scope/branch/repeat bounded graph, **not an interpreter**), and **12 synthetic spell-shaped fixtures**, not published canonical recipes. The main battle board **still has all 19 individually reviewed XPHB adapters**, which the new engine is required to preserve and compare against. \`SPELL_ENGINE_DECISIONS.md\` was already merged to main, so the architecture approval is recorded even though the runner is not installed.

Family taxonomy is **review metadata**; the recipe graph is a **proposed reusable execution description**; the future guarded server interpreter is a **separately gated implementation**. Mixing these three would falsely inflate automation coverage.
