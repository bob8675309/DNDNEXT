## What exists in this PR (including non-title work)

- **P1 non-executing recipe contract:** `utils/spellRecipes/validateSpellRecipe.mjs` restricts recipe JSON to source-qualified 2024 XPHB identities, typed actions, target geometry, damage/heal, bounded repeats and roll scopes. `preflightSpellRecipe.mjs` adds lexical repeat scope, DAG reachability, bounded worst-case work, shared-dice discipline and SHA-256 canonical recipe manifests. The recipes are synthetic structural examples, not 2024 gameplay approval.

- **Eldritch Blast tactical candidate:** `utils/eldritchBlastPlanner.mjs`, `utils/eldritchBlastStagingCast.mjs`, `components/encounter/EldritchBlastPlanner.js` and `pages/encounters/combat.js` explore independent beam attacks, character-level beam scaling, distinct beam target assignment and level-qualified invocation effects; proposed SQL remains under `sql/candidates/`, without public live casting.

- **Independent LOS/authorization evidence:** directional sight checks (including opposing adjacent caster/observer orientation), server-side source/feature eligibility, signed local GoTrue/PostgREST, PG16/17 source fixtures, lock timeout/contention and negative controls. A passing staged LOS fixture is not a deployed universal LOS contract.

- **Metadata/rules exceptions:** tactical known-spell assignment and mechanics review utilities document source/edition handling, 2024 per-spell exceptions and unsupported fallbacks. Original 19 deployed XPHB cast adapters are the regression baseline; do not replace them through this PR.
