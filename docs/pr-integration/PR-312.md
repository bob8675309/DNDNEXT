## What exists in this PR (including non-title work)

- **P2 spell shadow reference:** eight live/source reference spells and seven-spell review target, controlled Fire Bolt baseline, per-attack/server roll/AC, 2024 total-character-level cantrip scaling discrepancy, magic missile dart identity, spell geometry and selected-emanation behavior. `scripts/validate_spell_recipe_p2_shadow_contracts.mjs` and `...fire_bolt_shadow.mjs` are non-executing; no live spell executor was deployed.

- **Player-controlled NPCs, familiars and sidekicks:** `sql/candidates/20261101_npc_delegated_control_candidate.sql` proposes additive `character_permissions.can_control`, Admin-only grant/revoke, preserved independent `can_edit` and direct `controller_user_id`, server-controller checks and participant RLS. `NpcControlPermissionsPanel.js` + `/npcs` show Delegated ON / Other Access / No Access. Multiple independently authorized players may control one NPC but must share exactly one server-owned action/reaction/slot budget; one player may simultaneously manage several different actors.

- **Delegated spell and source visibility:** proposed v1 **and v2** `encounter_spellcasting_profile` wrappers restrict delegation-only users to **known and currently available spells** plus tactical state, not unprepared/unknown or GM-secret spells. The latter is the owner's confirmed decision. Original Admin/direct/source-authorized consumers retain their expected full responses. Do not weaken sheet, currency, inventory, character equipment effects or progression permissions when adding `can_control`. Tests detected a v2 bypass if only v1 was filtered.

- **Source-actual signed security tests:** local three-account GoTrue scenarios exercise admin-only grants, hidden-token participant visibility, unchanged-JWT revocation, two controllers racing for one Action, cached-result ownership and direct/indirect source RPC denials. One initial fixture incorrectly omitted enabling RLS and CI failed as intended; corrected fixture passes, but full production-schema and browser acceptance remain open.

- **Legacy command replay weakness:** original `encounter_use_core_action_v1` in scratch permits replay of another controller's Dash result under a reused request UUID, and return after revocation. `private.encounter_core_action_transaction_candidate_v1` demonstrates E-first then actor locking, immutable typed JSON `request_intent`, permission before cached replay, Dash/Dodge/Disengage parity, one-only ledger/log/version and fail-closed old pending rows. Live `request_intent` column and replacement RPC **do not exist**.

- **System-wide lock census:** 22 spell RPC names, other ledger writers and non-ledger mutations must adopt a coordinated lock order; `#312` also tracks reaction windows, character persistent slot synchronization, exact controller/hidden-result and UI wording. A stand-alone core action swap may introduce deadlock with legacy spell/weapon/turn paths.
