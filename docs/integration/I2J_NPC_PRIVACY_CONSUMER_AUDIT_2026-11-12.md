## Indirect server serializer and RLS compatibility

- **Stage1** tightens `character_sheets_select_visible_or_assigned` to `private.can_access_character_v1(character_id,'edit')` and defines allowlisted public `get_npc_profile_visible_v1`. The **initial Stage1 definition still has a `profileReveal.secret` branch that reads raw `characters.secret`**, making Stage1 alone NOT releasable.
- **Stage2** revokes direct authenticated `characters.background,motivation,quirk,mannerism,voice` SELECT. The new GM `get_npc_dossier_admin_v1` and current-editor `get_character_editor_narrative_v1` are separate `SECURITY DEFINER` paths; they must each authorize server-side, not rely on a client `isAdmin` boolean or row RLS.
- **Stage3** records on-insert creator provenance without backfilling guesses, guards non-GM player-authored GM secrets at INSERT, creates distinct creator-private Story and GM-published clue tables, restricts legacy `get_character_secrets_v1`/`set_character_secret_v1`, and replaces both `get_my_player_character_v1` and `get_my_player_characters_v2` to omit raw GM secret. Its final public Profile serializer uses only independently curated `character_public_secret_revelations.public_text` when revealed, never raw `characters.secret`.
- **#351 correction** binds the original private Story text (hashed transiently, final intent digest reserved) to the immutable creation nonce; a changed Story-only retry under the same nonce must be SQLSTATE `22023`, and an identical retry must never overwrite a later deliberate Story edit. The original #345 wrapper alone fails that invariant. Neither original red nor #351 signed green was rerun by I2J.
- The separate `public.players.sheet` Option A freeze is **not** satisfied by privacy RLS or by this partial composition. No historical snapshot may be overwritten, even `{}`.
