**Stock:** authenticated has character_stock SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE grants; the character_stock_admin_write RLS policy restricts writes through private.current_user_is_admin(), while ordinary signed SELECT is separately permitted subject to visible character. The disposable T28 shop fixture grants authenticated only SELECT, so it CANNOT prove live Admin restock, quantity editing, dumping stock, or route changes.

**Inconsistent Admin semantics:** private.current_user_is_admin() and public.is_admin() require EXACT role='admin'. The public is_admin(uid uuid) overload returns true when a caller queries self and the role is anything except player, subject to its target access check. Current town and inventory pages use the uid overload for isAdmin and can therefore render controls for a non-player role that does not satisfy strict Admin RLS. Source-original reroll_merchant_inventory_v2 checks exact admin; stock_merchant_item and wallet_set reject only role player. Role populations were not inspected, so this is a conditional but real contract mismatch, not an assertion that any specific GM experienced it.

**Wallet and buyer:** hosted wallet_get(uuid), wallet_set(uuid,numeric) are SECURITY DEFINER, authenticated-executable and anon-denied; their role rules are not identical to T28's local synthetic private.test_admins functions. Hosted original buy_from_merchant(uuid,uuid,integer) MD5 is 1dbe8c24c4c718396d7256f6f7465354, exactly the source-original SQL pinned in the signed fixture. Its authenticated EXECUTE is granted and anon EXECUTE denied. Genuine original buyer test provenance does NOT validate hosted wallet_set role behavior.

**Delegated inventory mismatch:** live inventory_items RLS SELECT/INSERT/UPDATE/DELETE checks user_id=auth.uid() OR strict Admin. Separately, private.can_access_character_v1(uuid,'inventory') admits per-character can_inventory OR can_edit, and public.get_character_inventory_v1(uuid) enforces it for SECURITY DEFINER reads. pages/inventory.js presents delegated NPC inventory management but fetches rows and writes directly with inventory_items table access, which does NOT inherit the privileged RPC permission. The candidate 20261105_inventory_character_owner_constraint_and_dml_review_v1.sql explicitly excludes direct delegated NPC/merchant transfer support. Do not grant all authenticated actors direct inventory access to paper over the mismatch.

**Inventory constraints:** hosted inventory_items has validated owner_type_check allowing player,npc,merchant,character, plus older NOT VALID owner_type_chk allowing only player,npc,merchant (NOT VALID still applies to new writes).
