## 2. Why UPDATE-only is insufficient and what the candidate actually proves

The historical original `sql/20260614_02_phase_a_non_map_security_hardening.sql` inventory RLS checks caller `user_id`, but its source does not constrain `owner_type/owner_id` in INSERT or UPDATE. A browser can potentially create a directly NPC-owned row with its own `user_id`, or update owner_type/owner_id while retaining `user_id`. This is a **source-backed risk**, NOT a verified hosted exploit or claim about installed RLS. P16's disposable invoker guard checks actual browser role for direct `owner_type='npc'` or merchant inserts while allowing manager-authorized item grants and source-original account/Town/Forge inserts. An explicit table-level UPDATE revoke is critical because **column REVOKE alone is ineffective if table UPDATE remains GRANTED**; only independently granted safe equipment columns remain writable.

The scratch grant list covers all named old RPCs observed in P13 and P15, including June `list_item_send_targets_v1` hidden/Forge target leakage and full-row return of `transfer_inventory_item_v1`, direct P2 single+bulk, direct P4 gift, old P6 manager approval, direct P6 offer without typed receipt, and unmapped original P8 batch.
