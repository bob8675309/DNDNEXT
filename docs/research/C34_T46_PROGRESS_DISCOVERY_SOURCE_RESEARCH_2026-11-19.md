## Owner policy: progress is a discoverable secret, not automatic public roster information

**Owner's explicit 2026-11-19 decision:** "**Restricted according to character visiablity (it could be tied to one of the secretes that gets revealed via dialogue or a good roll of the dice.)**"

Design interpretation for T46 (testable defaults, not a deployed behavior): a visible NPC/merchant may have a public-facing **sanitized identity/profile** but **does not automatically expose canonical class, level, precise XP, event history, private Sheet, private GM Secret or creator backstory**. A hidden character must not reveal even a previously granted progression clue to an unauthorized viewer. An exact `admin`, `gm` or `dm` current game role and explicit character `can_edit` retain full private progression/Sheet authority; `can_inventory`, `can_convert`, linked read-only, unrelated authenticated user and anonymous are not full-progression grants.

**Progression discovery is per player and per fact**. Game Master can grant/revoke `class`, `level`, `xp` independently for a specific authenticated player, optionally recording a GM-adjudicated `dialogue`, `ability_check` or `gm_direct` source and private note. This is distinct from a GM's hidden `characters.secret`, a published safe clue, and `character_sheets.sheet.profileReveal` (editable client JSON cannot authorize a private revelation). No automatic dice-result trigger or dialogue consumer is added in T46: the system must later verify/check a trusted campaign event or require explicit GM confirmation before granting. No party-wide broadcast is assumed; shared audience is a future separate policy/design decision. A currently unauthorized viewer receives nothing by default.
