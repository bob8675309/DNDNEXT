## Accepted owner behavior

1. A player can transfer items to **another player's account**, preserving ordinary player-to-player send, and pre-existing trade-request/approval workflows.
2. A player can **offer** an item to an independent/hired NPC; ownership does not change until an authorized NPC/GM decision **accepts**. Decline/cancel does not move items.
3. For a **fully delegated** companion, an explicit GM-approved `auto_accept_items` grant **and** valid current delegated controller/edit right may permit immediate item acceptance. Merely hiring, being friendly, viewing backpack or combat-control alone does not grant acceptance or withdrawal.
4. Source Forge character gear can be offered to controlled henchman/sidekick if current source-character inventory permission and item provenance are checked; same-account player-character-to-player-character transfers are **forbidden**, even though original Forge PCs have `characters.kind='npc'` with `player-character` tags.
5. Merchant stock `character_stock`, legitimate Forge starting equipment/crafting item INSERT, and original `trade_requests`/buyer RPCs have **different** authorization/settlement contracts; do not push them through NPC consent by accident.
6. Future NPC quests may author item requests or offers; accepting a request is not inventory/quest settlement. Quest credit/rewards require their own transactional authorization and idempotent receipt.

## Recommended versioned cutover service (design only)

Expose a **single transfer-intent dispatcher** accepting idempotent request UUID, an exact existing item ID, expected source owner type/ID, typed recipient scope and action context. Server derives actor from current `auth.uid()`, verifies current player/character permissions and real recipient taxonomy/visibility, and locks the item before NPC/recipient rows in consistent order across offers, equipment and bulk transfers. The client never supplies admin/GM, issuer NPC, story state, receipt or ownership flags as authority.

- **Target player account:** call the signed, source-owner-checked immediate account-to-account transfer preserving ordinary player sends. No NPC offer record required.
- **Target eligible independent NPC:** atomically record a private pending offer, no change to ownership/equip. A later authorized NPC/GM acceptance revalidates item/source/target and current permissions, commits once with receipt, then optional separately authorized quest credit.
- **Target fully delegated NPC:** auto-accept only if *both* current explicit `auto_accept_items` GM grant and current control/edit right exist; log accepted receipt with no pending ownership window.
- **Target tagged Forge PC:** deny any NPC route despite `kind='npc'` (even for manager callers), preserving owner's prohibition on same-account PC-to-PC.
- **Target merchant:** retain explicit canonical GM management or original merchant buyer commerce; do not automatically interpret merchant storefront stock as personal recipient bag.
- **Bulk operations:** either make a checked batch of pending offers or a checked batch of immediate player transfers as one atomic unit; never silently mix independent NPC consent and immediate ownership updates.
- **Old endpoints/permissions:** inventory UI switches to this authenticated service with **no direct table mutation fallback**; old direct NPC transfer/grants closed or delegated server-side only as part of an owner-reviewed schema/data migration. Must not break original legitimate Town, Forge, GM management, item creation, player trades, merchant stock or map-trigger contracts.
- **Observability:** include pending/accepted/declined/cancelled/expired state, signed source actor, recipient ID/typed scope, correlation/quest text ID if approved, and an authorized settlement receipt. No client role inference, no private card payload in result unless independently authorized.
